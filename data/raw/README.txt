OPEN-PIT ROCKFALL RISK DATASET
================================

Contents:
- 200 original synthetic top-view mine images
- 200 corresponding risk heatmap images
- 100 HIGH-risk samples
- 100 LOW-risk samples
- rockfall_dataset.csv
- rockfall_dataset.xlsx

Risk meaning:
HIGH (label 1): conditions indicate higher probability of rockfall.
LOW (label 0): conditions indicate lower probability of rockfall.

Important:
This is a SYNTHETIC dataset created for prototyping, model development,
UI/demo work, and testing. It is not a substitute for real mine survey,
LiDAR, photogrammetry, geotechnical measurements, or certified safety data.
